# Hardware troubleshooting

No boot: inspect 5 V, ground contact, capacitor polarity and pogo compression. PN532 absent: verify I2C mode/address, shared ground and voltage. Tag misses: increase tag/antenna parallel alignment and move magnet/metal away. Resets while sliding: observe input rail, improve contact/capacitance/feed. Stop immediately for heat, smell or low-resistance short. `REQUIRES_PHYSICAL_TEST`.
